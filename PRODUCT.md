# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Brands, businesses, and creators looking for a production partner for commercials, branded content, podcasts, events, and cinematic storytelling. They are usually evaluating trust, taste, and production quality before reaching out.

## Product Purpose
Filumed exists to turn interest into confidence: show that the studio can plan, shoot, and deliver polished visual storytelling with a strong cinematic point of view. Success looks like visitors quickly understanding the quality of the work and feeling ready to start a project.

## Brand Personality
Cinematic, premium, purposeful. The tone should feel confident and art-directed, with restraint rather than flashiness.

## Anti-references
Anything that feels like a generic AI-generated startup site, especially neon purple-blue gradients, overdesigned glassmorphism, or trendy motion that overwhelms the work. It should not feel playful, candy-colored, or synthetic.

## Design Principles
- Story before spectacle.
- Cinematic restraint beats decorative noise.
- Every interaction should reinforce craft and confidence.
- Motion should feel intentional, tactile, and filmic.
- The red accent is a signal, not a flood.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target clear, readable contrast across light and dark sections, keep accent usage supportive rather than essential for comprehension, and preserve reduced-motion fallbacks for animated surfaces.
